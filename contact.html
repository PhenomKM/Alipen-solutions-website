Procurement & Delivery FAQs

How fast is hospital delivery in Kenya?

Facilities within Nairobi and surrounding counties receive same-day or next-morning dispatch. Regional deliveries to Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, and Eldoret arrive within 24 to 48 hours.

Do you issue official tax and proforma invoices?

Yes, Alipen Solutions Agency Ltd is a registered corporate entity in Kenya. We issue compliant ETR proforma invoices and delivery notes.

Are bulk institutional discounts available?

Yes, we offer volume pricing tiers for hospitals ordering case quantities of ultrasound gel, cartons of dry X-ray films, or multiple patient monitors.
