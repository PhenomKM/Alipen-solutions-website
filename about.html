Our Company Story & Purpose

Healthcare Excellence with a Trusted Partner

Alipen Solutions Agency Ltd is committed to bridging the gap between world-class medical innovation and everyday clinical practice across Kenya.

Headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, Alipen Solutions Agency Ltd was founded with a clear directive: to ensure that healthcare professionals have immediate, uninterrupted access to certified diagnostic devices, dependable imaging media, essential surgical consumables, and durable hospital furniture.

Under the executive leadership of Peter Ogettoh, we work hand-in-hand with hospital procurement heads, chief medical officers, and diagnostic specialists to supply high-reliability inventory that protects patients, enables timely clinical diagnosis, and optimizes facility operating budgets.

Medical Logistics Infrastructure

Centralized Nairobi Supply Hub with National Dispatch

To eliminate stockouts in Kenyan healthcare facilities, Alipen Solutions maintains active buffer inventory of high-demand consumables and equipment in our Nairobi facility.

  • ✓ Barcoded lot tracking and expiration monitoring for sterile consumables
  • ✓ Same-day emergency courier dispatch for Nairobi and surrounding metro hospitals
  • ✓ Secured road and regional transit serving Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, and all 47 counties
Biomedical Engineering & Compliance

Rigorous Pre-Deployment Testing & Technical Calibration

Patient safety depends on diagnostic accuracy. Every multi-parameter patient monitor and oxygen concentrator is verified by biomedical technicians before handoff to medical teams.

  • ✓ Vital signs calibration (ECG waveforms, NIBP pressure sensors, SpO2 pulse oximetry)
  • ✓ Oxygen purity level validation (>93% ± 3% check with calibrated oxygen analyzers)
  • ✓ Installation assistance, user manuals, and technical advisory for hospital bio-engineers
